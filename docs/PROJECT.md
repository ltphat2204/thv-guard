## Thesis

Permission to call a tool is not approval of the exact business transaction. A valid refund must satisfy balance limits, approval requirements, exact parameter binding, expiry, and replay constraints. Similar questions arise in procurement and finance, but refund is the only detailed interactive example.

The intended verification boundary complements existing IAM, authorization and application controls. A policy decision is not successful or safe execution; atomic execution, stale-state verification, replay protection, bypass prevention and audit integrity remain future engineering work.
